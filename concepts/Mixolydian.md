# Mixolydian

The Mixolydian mode is the fifth mode of the major scale, spanning the fifth scale degree to the fifth scale degree an octave higher. In C major, Mixolydian spans G to G on all white keys and contains a major third, perfect fourth, perfect fifth, and minor seventh—the sound of a dominant seventh chord. Mixolydian is used over V chords (e.g., G7) and is the primary scale choice for dominant seventh chords resolving to major chords. Unlike altered dominants, unaltered Mixolydian is consonant and stable, making it the default choice when a dominant leads to a major chord. The natural ninth and thirteenth in Mixolydian create open extensions on the dominant chord.
