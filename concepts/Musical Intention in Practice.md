# Musical Intention in Practice

The principle that technical exercises should be approached with the same intentionality and expressiveness as performance — not as mechanical repetition but as music-making. A specific technique is to mentally cast a favourite musician as the performer of the exercise, hearing their ideal sound in the inner ear before attempting to approximate it on the instrument.

## Appearances

- **[[../videos/0paCMVrPm_8|Five Levels of Pentatonic Scale Practice]]** [▶ 9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0paCMVrPm_8&t=583s) — introduced during Level 1, after the basic scale-running exercise is established. Maness asks players to imagine Herbie Hancock, Pat Metheny, Charlie Parker, or another admired musician playing the same pentatonic figure, and to let that imagined sound guide the physical execution. The frame shifts practice from drilling notes to cultivating an ideal sonic image.
