# Pocket

The pocket is the precise rhythmic space where a groove lives—the place where all members of a rhythm section lock in together and create the foundation that makes people dance. Staying in the pocket means resisting the urge to add fills, improvise, or do anything beyond serving the groove and the band. For pianists and keyboardists trained in straight-ahead jazz, where individual harmonic freedom is the norm, learning to play in the pocket requires surrendering personal expression in favor of supporting the collective rhythm section. Pocket practice involves looping a single groove for an extended, sometimes uncomfortable duration until the body and ear understand the micro-rhythmic variations that make the groove feel alive and human—the anticipations, the ghost notes, the subtle pushes and pulls that all major groove artists employ while staying true to the fundamental rhythm.
