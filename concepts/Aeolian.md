# Aeolian

The Aeolian mode is the sixth mode of the major scale, spanning the sixth scale degree to the sixth scale degree an octave higher. In C major, Aeolian spans A to A on all white keys and contains a minor third, perfect fourth, perfect fifth, and minor seventh—the natural minor scale. Aeolian is used over vi chords (e.g., A minor seven) within a diatonic framework and provides the brightest of the minor modes because it contains only one accidental relative to the major scale (the lowered third). In jazz improvisation, Aeolian is the diatonic choice for minor seventh chords in a major key context, offering a stable, consonant minor sound that does not introduce exotic intervals like the flat-two of Phrygian or the diminished character of Locrian.
