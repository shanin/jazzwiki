# Guide Tone Motion

The principle that the third and seventh of a chord (the "guide tones" or "shell notes") naturally resolve to the seventh and third of the following chord with minimal movement — often by a half step or whole step. Because these two intervals swap roles between adjacent chords in a ii–V–I or circle-of-fifths progression, a pianist using rootless voicings can hold or move just one note while the other stays, creating smooth, almost inevitable-sounding voice leading without planning individual notes.
