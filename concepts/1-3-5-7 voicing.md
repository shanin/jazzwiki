# 1-3-5-7 voicing

The 1-3-5-7 voicing framework is a foundational approach to building harmony that relies on the basic triad extensions rather than scale-dependent alterations. Instead of using modes or scales like Dorian, Mixolydian, or altered scales to determine which tensions to add to a chord, this method constructs all voicings from the essential four-note structure: root (1), major or minor third (3), perfect or diminished fifth (5), and the seventh (7). Each of these four notes can be modified or extended by hinging, creating natural voice-leading and tension without adding extraneous pitches. This approach keeps the fundamental 1-3-5-7 always represented in the voicing, providing harmonic clarity and balance. The 1-3-5-7 structure is particularly valuable for improvisation and reharmonization because it is intuitive, flexible, and independent of specific scalar frameworks.
