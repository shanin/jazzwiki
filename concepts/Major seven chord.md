# Major seven chord

A major seven chord contains a major third and a major seventh interval above the root. In voicing applications, rootless major seven voicings typically build from the third upward through fourths, culminating in the major third interval at the top of the voicing. The major seven chord is foundational to jazz harmony and appears frequently in standards and modern compositions.
