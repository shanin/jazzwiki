# Practice Mindfulness

The practice of paying deliberate attention to physical and mental states during a practice session, treating fatigue, tension, and distraction not as obstacles to push through but as information — signals that a break, a reset, or a change of approach is needed. Good musicians know when to stop and reset as part of the discipline of practice, not a failure of it.
