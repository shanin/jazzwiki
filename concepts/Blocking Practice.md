# Blocking Practice

Blocking practice is a technique for developing hand position and coordination between the hands before attempting to play a passage at full speed. Rather than immediately playing notes in their intended rhythmic sequence, the pianist first plays all notes of a given hand together (as a block or chord), establishing the correct hand shape and finger placement. Once this position is internalized, the notes are then broken up and played in their actual rhythmic pattern. This approach is particularly valuable for scalar runs and complex passages where hand positioning is critical to achieving speed and evenness. By separating position from rhythm, a player can develop clean technique without the distraction of trying to coordinate timing prematurely.
