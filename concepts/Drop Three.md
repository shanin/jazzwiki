# Drop Three

Drop three voicing is created by taking the third note from the top of a closed voicing and dropping it down an octave into the left hand. This creates the widest spread among the three primary voicing structures and brings out richness in the tenor register. Drop three is particularly effective for creating space to accompany other instruments or voices and produces a distinctly different harmonic color compared to closed voicings and drop two.
