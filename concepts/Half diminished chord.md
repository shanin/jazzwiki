# Half diminished chord

A half diminished chord, also called minor seven flat five, contains a minor third, diminished fifth, and dominant seventh interval above the root. In traditional bebop voicing, a half diminished chord is often voiced as a minor triad rooted a major third above the bass note (for example, E flat minor over C bass for a C half diminished). Modern rootless voicings approach the half diminished as an upper structure triad: a minor six-nine voicing over the root note works reliably in most musical contexts.
