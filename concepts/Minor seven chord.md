# Minor seven chord

A minor seven chord contains a minor third and a dominant seventh (minor seventh) interval above the root. Widely used in jazz as both a functional harmony and as a stable chord quality, minor seven chords frequently appear in blues, modal vamps, and standards. Rootless voicings of minor seven chords can be constructed with the root and eleventh in the left hand and seventh, third, and fifth in the right hand, or alternatively built as inversions in diatonic fourths.
