# Major 7 chord

A major 7 chord is built on a major triad (root, major 3rd, perfect 5th) with the addition of a major 7th, giving it a bright, sophisticated character. The major 7 interval creates harmonic stability without the brightness of a natural ninth. In jazz, major 7 chords are fundamental to ii-V-I progressions and are often voiced using the Root Shell Pretty framework, with the shell (3rd and 7th) and upper extensions (9th, 11th, or 13th) distributed across the hands.
