# 2-5-1 progression

The 2-5-1 progression is a fundamental chord sequence in jazz consisting of the ii, V, and I chords (in root position, typically ii-7, V7, Imaj7). In the key of C, this is D minor 7, G7, C major 7. The 2-5-1 is one of the most common progressions in jazz standards and provides an ideal framework for practicing harmonic concepts because each chord has a clear functional role: the ii chord establishes a minor tonality, the V chord builds tension, and the I chord resolves. It is frequently used as a practice vehicle for learning scales, voicings, improvisation, and substitutions. In this context, it serves as the foundation for learning tritone substitutions, as it offers clear harmonic landmarks where tritone subs can be applied and heard distinctly.
