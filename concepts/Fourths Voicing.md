# Fourths Voicing

Fourths voicings are constructed by stacking successive perfect fourth intervals vertically. They can be four-note, five-note, or six-note structures, and may be built diatonically within a mode or key. These voicings are valued for their ability to transpose smoothly across diatonic sequences without requiring reharmonization, making them especially useful in modal contexts. Fourths voicings often produce striking harmonic colors, particularly when tritones emerge naturally from the interval structure.
