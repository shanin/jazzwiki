# Dominant seven chord

A dominant seven chord contains a major third and a dominant seventh (minor seventh) interval above the root. Often used as a V chord in functional harmony, the dominant seven chord creates tension that resolves downward by semitone. In rootless voicing applications, dominant seven voicings are built similarly to major seven voicings but with the dominant seventh instead of the major seventh, often incorporating a tritone interval at the bottom of certain inversions.
