# So-What Voicing

A so-what voicing is a fourths voicing topped with a major third (or occasionally a minor third), creating a hybrid structure that combines the transpositional fluidity of fourths with a defined harmonic anchor. Named for its prominent use in modal jazz contexts, so-what voicings are easily moved diatonically across chord progressions and modes without alteration. When played on white keys in D minor, for example, a so-what voicing can be shifted up the scale to fit other harmonic contexts, making it a favorite of pianists despite its potential for overuse.
