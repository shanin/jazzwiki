# Ionian

The Ionian mode is the first mode of the major scale, the parent scale itself. In C major, Ionian spans C to C on all white keys and contains all the consonant intervals of the major scale: major thirds, perfect fifths, and a major seventh. In jazz improvisation, Ionian is used over major seventh chords (e.g., C major seven) and establishes the tonal center of a tune. While other modes add color and tension, Ionian remains the home base—the mode that defines a key's fundamental sound.
