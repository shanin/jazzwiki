# Moo chord

The moo chord is a modern-sounding chord voicing and substitution technique that combines a triad over an upper third. In a 3-6-2-5 progression, this voicing is constructed as a D add 2 triad (D, E, A) placed over F sharp (the third of D major), creating a sophisticated and contemporary harmonic color. The moo chord is a cousin of the secondary dominant and creates beautiful half-step movement into the following chord. It is particularly associated with modern jazz and the harmonic language of artists like Steely Dan. The voicing is built entirely on 1-3-5-7 hinging principles and maintains the essential harmonic structure while delivering a unique and elegant sound. The bass note is attached to this voicing, distinguishing it from other substitutions where the bass is detached.
