# Clavinet

A clavinet is an electric keyboard instrument that produces a distinctive woody, percussive sound by using a mechanical plucking mechanism similar to a harpsichord. Popular in funk and soul music from the 1960s onward, the clavinet (particularly the Hohner D6 model) became essential to the sound of classic grooves. Unlike the Rhodes piano or Fender Rhodes, which have a warmer, more sustained tone, the clavinet has an immediate attack and natural percussive character that makes it ideal for locked-in groove work. Its sound sits well in a funk rhythm section where the keys player must support the groove rather than dominate it.
