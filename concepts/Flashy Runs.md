# Flashy Runs

Flashy runs are showy scalar passages designed to sound technically impressive while remaining ergonomical and playable at fast tempos. These runs prioritize hand position and fingering patterns that fit naturally under the fingers, allowing them to be executed quickly without tension. Rather than being show-off techniques divorced from musicality, effective runs like those famously played by Art Tatum serve musical purposes—adding flourish and excitement to harmonic progressions while sounding effortless and flowing. The key to playing them convincingly is finding repeatable hand shapes and rhythmic patterns that can be internalized and then executed at whatever speed the music demands.
