# Four-way Close

Four-way close voicing is a fundamental four-note closed voicing structure that serves as the parent voicing for both Locked Hands and Drop Two voicing systems. In four-way close, all notes of a chord are stacked in compact form in the upper register, with no large gaps between voices. This structure provides the starting point for deriving more complex voicing systems: adding the top note an octave below creates Locked Hands, while dropping the second voice from the top an octave lower produces Drop Two.
