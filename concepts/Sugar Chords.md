# Sugar Chords

Sugar chords are a method of creating rich, sophisticated harmonic textures by systematically combining three voicing structures: closed voicings, drop two, and drop three. By mixing and matching these structures within a single passage, musicians achieve beautiful voice leading with both contrary and parallel motion while maintaining flexibility across different registers. The technique is particularly valuable in solo piano where creating textural variety and managing both melody and harmony demands sophisticated voicing choices.
