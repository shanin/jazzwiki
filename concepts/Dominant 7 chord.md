# Dominant 7 chord

A dominant 7 chord (V7) combines a major triad (root, major 3rd, perfect 5th) with a minor 7th, creating a hybrid sound between the major 7 and minor 7 chord qualities. The dominant 7 chord is the strongest chord function in tonal harmony, pulling powerfully toward the tonic. In jazz contexts, it has a bluesy, edgy character that works in dominant-seventh phrases (V7-I) as well as secondary dominants leading to other chord tones. When voiced using Root Shell Pretty, the shell contains the major 3rd and the minor 7th, creating the characteristic bluesy tension.
