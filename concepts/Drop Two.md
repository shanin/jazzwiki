# Drop Two

Drop two voicing is created by taking the second note from the top of a closed voicing and dropping it down an octave into the left hand. This structure creates a wide spread between the upper and lower voices and is often described as a "tenths delivery device" because the right hand's top note and the left hand's drop two note typically form a beautiful tenth interval. Drop two voicings are particularly effective for creating spacious, elegant textures and are fundamental to sophisticated voice leading.
