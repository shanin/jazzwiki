# Locrian

The Locrian mode is the seventh mode of the major scale, spanning the seventh scale degree to the seventh scale degree an octave higher. In C major, Locrian spans B to B on all white keys and contains a minor third, diminished fifth, and minor seventh—forming a half-diminished seventh chord (minor seven flat five). Locrian is the darkest and most unstable of the seven modes due to its diminished fifth, which creates tritone tension. In jazz, Locrian is used over vii chords (e.g., B minor seven flat five) within a diatonic framework. The diminished quality makes Locrian function as a leading-tone scale that creates forward harmonic motion and is rarely used as an extended improvisation vehicle; instead, it provides color and tension on the vii chord before resolution.
