# Voice Leading

Voice leading refers to the smooth, musical progression of individual melodic lines within a harmonic texture. Effective voice leading minimizes large jumps between notes, creates logical connections between chord tones, and can generate both contrary motion (where lines move in opposite directions) and parallel motion. By mixing and matching different voicing structures, musicians can create sophisticated inner-voice movement that adds dimension and musical interest to harmonic progressions.
