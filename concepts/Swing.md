# Swing

Swing is the forward-moving, lilting quality of jazz that results from specific rhythmic placements, emphasis, and confidence. It is not a technique that can be written on a page or explained analytically; instead, it must be felt and internalized through listening and playing. Swing emerges from the combination of syncopation, timing nuances, and conviction in phrasing. A musician with strong swing can play simple, even sparse material and sound far more compelling than a musician playing complex harmonies without swing. Swing is achieved through embodied practice—singing rhythms, listening to great musicians, and developing confidence in rhythmic placement. It is often described as dancing the rhythm, as opposed to thinking or calculating it.
