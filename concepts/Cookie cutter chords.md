# Cookie cutter chords

Cookie cutter chords are common, generic voicings used by virtually every jazz pianist because they sound beautiful and work in almost any situation. The term reflects the metaphor that these voicings can be applied anywhere like a cookie cutter mold—as long as you know which voicing goes over which chord, confident execution alone produces great results without needing to understand the underlying harmonic reasoning. These voicings are called "cookie cutter" not as a criticism but as an acknowledgment of their universality and reliability.
