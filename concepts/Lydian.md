# Lydian

The Lydian mode is the fourth mode of the major scale, spanning the fourth scale degree to the fourth scale degree an octave higher. In C major, Lydian spans F to F on all white keys and contains a major third, augmented fourth (raised fourth or sharp 11), perfect fifth, and major seventh—the sound of a major seventh chord with a built-in sharp 11 extension. In jazz, Lydian is used over IV chords (e.g., F major seven) within a diatonic framework. The raised fourth degree gives Lydian a bright, open, almost ethereal quality that distinguishes it from Ionian. The natural sharp 11 makes Lydian ideal for improvisers seeking a colorful major sound without explicitly playing an alteration.
