# Circle of 4ths

The circle of 4ths is the reverse perspective of the circle of 5ths, moving counter-clockwise and progressing by perfect 4ths instead of 5ths. In jazz practice, moving through keys by 4ths (like C, F, B flat, E flat) creates a descending harmonic motion that feels natural and is often used in chord progressions. The circle of 4ths is employed as an alternative practice strategy for learning voicings and harmonic material across multiple keys, offering different intervallic logic than chromatic or circle-of-5ths approaches.
