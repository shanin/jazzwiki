# Harmonic minor scale

The harmonic minor scale is the natural minor scale with a raised seventh degree, creating a leading tone that emphasizes the harmonic pull back to the tonic. In jazz, the harmonic minor scale serves two primary functions: as a soloing option over minor chords (providing color through chromatic passing tones and the raised seventh), and as the parent scale for deriving dominant alterations. When building scale choices over a secondary dominant chord, musicians can use the harmonic minor scale of the resolution target transposed to the root of the dominant. For example, over an F7 chord resolving to B-flat minor, the B-flat harmonic minor scale starting from F provides a cohesive set of color tones that acknowledge both the dominant function and the destination tonality.
