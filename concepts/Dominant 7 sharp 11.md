# Dominant 7 sharp 11

A dominant 7 sharp 11 is a tritone substitution voicing that combines a dominant 7 chord with a raised fourth degree (sharp 11). This voicing is built on the Lydian dominant scale and is the primary sonic tool for tritone substitutions. The raised fourth prevents the chord from sounding like it belongs to a specific major key and instead creates an ambiguous, suspended quality that resolves into the target chord. The shared intervals between a dominant 7 and the tritone sub make the dominant 7 sharp 11 the most effective and most natural-sounding tritone sub voicing. Pianists and other harmonic instruments use the dominant 7 sharp 11 as the foundation for tritone sub comping and improvisation, while improvisers navigate it using the underlying Lydian dominant scale.
