# Contrary Motion

Contrary motion occurs when melodic lines move in opposite directions simultaneously—typically the outer voices of a harmonic texture moving up while inner voices move down or vice versa. In the context of sugar chords, contrary motion is created by strategically alternating between voicing structures, allowing the outside notes to move in opposite directions while maintaining sophisticated inner-voice movement. This technique adds depth and visual/auditory interest to harmonic progressions.
