# Magic Voicings

Rootless, two-handed voicings built from major pentatonic shapes, designed for comping behind a full ensemble (horns, vocalist, orchestra). Because the root is omitted and no notes are doubled, the voicings sit in a clean upper register and cut through a dense texture without muddying the bass. The five notes of the pentatonic scale are spread between the hands with strategic skips. Smooth voice leading emerges naturally by moving through a single parent pentatonic scale rather than jumping between unrelated shapes.

## Appearances

- **[[../videos/-ktm1Q0odH8|Three Jazz Piano Voicing Structures for Lady Bird]]** [▶ 15:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ktm1Q0odH8&t=920s) — introduced as the third and most demanding of the three voicing systems, used when accompanying a full band. All five notes of the major pentatonic are distributed between the hands; a specific E♭7♯11♭9 voicing attributed to Peter Martin is used as an example of the extended upper-structure sounds the system can produce.
