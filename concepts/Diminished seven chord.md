# Diminished seven chord

A diminished seven chord contains a minor third, diminished fifth, and double flat seven (enharmonically equivalent to a major sixth) above the root. Built from the diminished scale (whole-half diminished scale), the diminished seven chord exhibits remarkable symmetry—its intervals divide the octave equally, creating three additional exact inversions that maintain identical interval relationships. This symmetry makes diminished seven voicings unusually versatile and aesthetically striking.
