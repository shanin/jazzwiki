# Circle of 5ths

The circle of 5ths is a visual and conceptual tool representing the relationship between all twelve chromatic pitches and their corresponding key signatures. Moving clockwise around the circle advances in intervals of a perfect 5th (or a 4th counter-clockwise), making it a natural organizing system for chord progressions and practice routines. In jazz pedagogy, the circle of 5ths is used as a systematic way to practice voicings and harmonic material across multiple keys with logical harmonic relationships, developing both finger facility and harmonic understanding.
