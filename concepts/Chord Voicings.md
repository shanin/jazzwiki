# Chord Voicings

Chord voicings are the art of arranging the tones of a chord across different registers and hand positions on the piano. Rather than playing chord tones in fixed intervals, jazz pianists choose voicings based on context: whether playing solo, accompanying a rhythm section, or comping for a band. The selection and spacing of chord tones dramatically affect the sound's color, balance, and ability to support a melody or blend with an ensemble.
