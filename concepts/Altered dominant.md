# Altered dominant

An altered dominant chord is a dominant seven chord with raised or lowered extensions (typically sharp nine, flat nine, sharp 11, or flat 13), creating a tense, chromatic sound that resolves effectively. Voicings for altered dominants often employ upper structure triads over the bass note—for example, an A flat major triad over a C bass yields a C7 altered sound. Remarkably, altered dominant voicings frequently serve double duty as tritone substitutions: the same voicing can function as both a C7 altered chord and a G flat seven sharp 11 chord.
