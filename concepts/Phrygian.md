# Phrygian

The Phrygian mode is the third mode of the major scale, spanning the third scale degree to the third scale degree an octave higher. In C major, Phrygian spans E to E on all white keys and contains a minor third, perfect fourth, perfect fifth, and minor seventh—forming a minor seventh chord with a characteristic flat-two degree. Phrygian has an exotic, Spanish-influenced color due to its lowered second interval. In jazz, Phrygian is used over iii chords (e.g., E minor seven) within a diatonic framework. The flat-two interval distinguishes Phrygian from Dorian and Aeolian and creates harmonic tension and motion when in this minor setting.
