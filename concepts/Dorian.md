# Dorian

The Dorian mode is the second mode of the major scale, spanning the second scale degree to the second scale degree an octave higher. In C major, Dorian spans D to D on all white keys and contains a minor third, perfect fourth, perfect fifth, and minor seventh—the sound of a minor seventh chord. In jazz improvisation, Dorian is used over ii chords (e.g., D minor seven) within a diatonic harmony framework. Dorian is a minor sound but differs from Aeolian (natural minor) by having a natural sixth degree, which gives it a brighter, more open character suited to the diatonic ii chord.
