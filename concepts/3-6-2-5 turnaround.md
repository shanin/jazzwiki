# 3-6-2-5 turnaround

The 3-6-2-5 turnaround is one of the most ubiquitous chord progressions in all of music. Built from diatonic seventh chords in a given key center, the progression creates a smooth harmonic journey through the scale degrees and typically resolves back to the I chord or vamps back and forth. In F major, it consists of A minor 7 (III), D minor 7 (VI), G minor 7 (II), and C7 (V), resolving to F major. The progression is beloved because it is both beautiful to listen to and deeply satisfying to play. Its harmonic structure supports countless jazz standards and Great American Songbook tunes. The widespread use of 3-6-2-5 makes it an ideal candidate for harmonic substitution and reharmonization, allowing musicians to add personal voice and sophistication while maintaining the progression's essential character.
