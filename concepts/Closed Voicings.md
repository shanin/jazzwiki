# Closed Voicings

Closed voicings pack all chord tones (third, fifth, seventh, ninth) as tightly as possible within a single octave or across two hands. This structure produces a warm, integrated sound reminiscent of saxophone section voicing. In solo piano playing, closed voicings are essential when hands must remain close together to produce a punchy, intimate texture, particularly in lower registers or when creating a cohesive harmonic block.
