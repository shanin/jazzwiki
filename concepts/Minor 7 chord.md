# Minor 7 chord

A minor 7 chord is built on a minor triad (root, minor 3rd, perfect 5th) with the addition of a minor 7th, creating a darker, moodier quality than the major 7 chord. This chord quality is ubiquitous in jazz, appearing as the ii chord in ii-V-I progressions and as a foundational voicing for minor-key jazz standards. When voiced using Root Shell Pretty, the shell contains the minor 3rd and the minor 7th, and the pretty notes often include extensions like the 9th and 11th.
