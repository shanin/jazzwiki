# Comping Rhythm

The rhythmic placement of chords during accompaniment. Rather than landing voicings squarely on the downbeat, experienced jazz pianists "comp" by placing chords on anticipations and off-beats that propel the groove forward. One well-known model is the **Red Garland feel**: anticipating the next chord change by striking it on beat 4 (or beat 2) of the preceding bar, locking the comping into the rhythmic flow of the rhythm section.
