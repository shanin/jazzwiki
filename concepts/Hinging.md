# Hinging

Hinging is a voicing technique where one note of a 1-3-5-7 chord structure is altered or extended to create harmonic color and voice leading without adding extraneous pitches outside the chord. The root can hinge up to any natural 9, flat 9, or sharp 9. The fifth can hinge down to a flat 5 or up to a 13. The third can hinge up to a fourth, creating a suspended third effect. Because the basic 1-3-5-7 structure remains represented, hinging preserves harmonic clarity while allowing for sophisticated alterations. This creates a sense of balance and forward momentum in four-part harmony, as hinged notes provide a direction and destination for voice movement. Hinging is particularly effective for creating smooth voice leading between chords and for building tension in harmonic progressions while maintaining tonal grounding.
