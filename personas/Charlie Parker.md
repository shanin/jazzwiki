# Charlie Parker

Charlie Parker (1920–1955) was an alto saxophonist and composer who revolutionized jazz through the invention of bebop in the 1940s. His harmonic innovations, asymmetrical phrasing, and virtuosic command of the saxophone established new standards for improvisation and influenced virtually every jazz musician who followed. Parker was a legendary practitioner of deep transcription and ear-training, embodying the principle of extracting music directly from recordings and internalizing it completely.
