# Charlie Parker

Charlie Parker (1920–1955), known universally as "Bird," was the defining voice of bebop saxophone and one of the most consequential figures in jazz history. His harmonic innovations — rapid chord substitutions, chromatic passing tones, sophisticated use of upper extensions — reshaped jazz improvisation permanently. His compositions (*Donna Lee*, *Ornithology*, *Ko-Ko*, *Confirmation*) remain core repertoire.
