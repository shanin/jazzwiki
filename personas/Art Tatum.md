# Art Tatum

Art Tatum was a virtuoso jazz pianist of the swing era, renowned for his extraordinary technical facility and inventive harmonic language. His playing was characterized by rapid scalar runs, sophisticated chord voicings, and the ability to accompany himself with complex left-hand patterns. Tatum's approach to the standards repertoire set a benchmark for pianistic excellence and harmonic sophistication that influenced generations of jazz musicians. His runs and flourishes demonstrate the marriage of technical showmanship and musical substance.
