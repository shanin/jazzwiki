# Chick Corea

Chick Corea (1941–2021) was a virtuoso pianist, composer, and bandleader who bridged post-bop, fusion, and contemporary jazz. Known for his technical brilliance, harmonic innovation, and leadership of influential ensembles, Corea stands among the most recorded and performed jazz composers of all time. He is frequently cited as an example of a master musician whose work merits deep transcription study.
