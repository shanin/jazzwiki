# Donald Fagan

Donald Fagan is a pianist, vocalist, and composer best known as the co-founder and primary songwriter of the jazz-rock band Steely Dan. His sophisticated harmonic sensibilities and meticulous approach to composition have made Steely Dan a touchstone of modern jazz harmony, influencing generations of jazz musicians and educators. Fagan is known for specific harmonic innovations, including the Mu chord (an E major triad with an added ninth), which has become a studied voicing technique in contemporary jazz piano pedagogy.
