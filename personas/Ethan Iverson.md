# Ethan Iverson

Ethan Iverson is a pianist, composer, and music writer known for his scholarly approach to jazz history and technique. He has written extensively about the history of jazz voicings and pianists' approaches to comping and harmony. His blog contains detailed analyses of specific pianists' techniques and approaches, including works examining Red Garland's distinctive voicing methodology.
