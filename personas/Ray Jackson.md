# Ray Jackson

Ray Jackson is a keyboardist best known for his iconic clavinet performance on Bill Withers' Use Me. His simple yet compelling two-chord (Em7 to A7) riff in octaves became one of the most famous and influential clavinet parts in soul and funk history. Jackson's contribution exemplifies the art of groove playing: the written part is straightforward, but the feel, the pocket, and the commitment to serving the song are what make it transcendent.
