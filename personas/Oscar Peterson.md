# Oscar Peterson

Oscar Peterson was a Canadian jazz pianist known for his technical virtuosity, harmonic sophistication, and prolific recording career. Peterson's playing combined swing, blues, and bebop influences with a distinctive touch and rhythmic drive. He was equally comfortable as a solo pianist, trio accompanist, or in jam sessions with leading soloists. Peterson's approach to improvisation and his ability to play complex passage work at high speeds made him one of the most respected and influential pianists in post-bop jazz.
