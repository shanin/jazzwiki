# Robert Glasper

A contemporary virtuoso pianist and composer, Robert Glasper stands among the most gifted and influential jazz musicians of the current era. His philosophy of learning music emphasizes extracting the essence directly from recordings through ear alone, without relying on written transcriptions, reflecting a deep commitment to the oral traditions of jazz and funk.

- **[[../videos/oRdkepq3ARk|The Easy Way To Get Good At Music]]** [▶ 4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oRdkepq3ARk&t=286s) — shares his approach to learning by ear: listening to records, internalizing them, and writing them out himself rather than reading transcriptions, demonstrating that dedication and direct engagement with recordings is essential to reaching the highest level
