# Adam Maness

Adam Maness is a jazz pianist and educator known for his systematic approach to teaching jazz harmony and voicing techniques. As the founder and primary instructor of Open Studio, he specializes in breaking down complex piano concepts into clear, pedagogical frameworks. His teaching emphasizes fundamental structures like Root Shell Pretty and Magic Voicings, making advanced jazz piano techniques accessible to students at various levels.
