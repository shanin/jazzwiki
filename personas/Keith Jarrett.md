# Keith Jarrett

Keith Jarrett (b. 1945) is one of the most celebrated jazz pianists of his generation, known for his solo piano concerts (including the landmark *Köln Concert*), his Standards Trio recordings with Gary Peacock and Jack DeJohnette, and an uncompromising commitment to spontaneous creation. His playing draws on jazz, classical, gospel, and folk traditions, characterised by an almost vocal approach to melodic line and an intense physical and emotional engagement with the instrument.
