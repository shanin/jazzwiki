# Buddy Bolden

Buddy Bolden was a legendary early jazz cornet player and bandleader from New Orleans, credited with inventing the "big four" rhythmic pattern that became foundational to jazz. While Bolden's own recordings are not known to exist, his historical significance rests on his role in developing the syncopated, swinging approach to rhythm that distinguished jazz from earlier march and ragtime traditions. He is remembered as the originator of the rhythmic concept where the fourth beat of a measure is accented, creating the forward-moving lilt characteristic of jazz. Bolden's innovations in rhythmic vocabulary mark a pivotal moment in American musical history—the transition from straight march time to the syncopated, swinging rhythm of jazz.
