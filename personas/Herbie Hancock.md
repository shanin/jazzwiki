# Herbie Hancock

Herbie Hancock (b. 1940) is a pianist, composer, and keyboardist whose work spans post-bop, funk, and contemporary jazz. A prolific innovator known for synthesizer exploration and harmonic sophistication, Hancock has profoundly influenced multiple generations of jazz musicians. He is frequently cited as a master whose work merits close study and transcription.
