# Herbie Hancock

Herbie Hancock (b. 1940) is one of the most influential jazz pianists and composers of the 20th and 21st centuries. Associated with Miles Davis's Second Great Quintet in the 1960s, he later pioneered jazz-funk fusion and continues to record and perform at the highest level. His piano style blends bebop harmonic fluency with modal openness, rhythmic precision, and an ear for texture and space.
