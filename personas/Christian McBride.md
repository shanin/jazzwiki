# Christian McBride

A virtuoso acoustic bassist and passionate educator, Christian McBride is widely regarded as one of the finest bass players and teachers working in jazz today. His approach to teaching emphasizes the primacy of feel, phrasing, and internalizing music over mechanical note-learning, grounding students in the emotional and physical realities of playing jazz rather than the abstractions of music notation.
