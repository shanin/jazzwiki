# Bill Withers

Bill Withers is a soul and funk singer-songwriter whose song Use Me features one of the most iconic clavinet riffs in popular music. Withers' approach to songwriting emphasizes simplicity, groove, and emotional directness. The sparse arrangement of Use Me—a two-chord progression built on Ray Jackson's clavinet part—demonstrates that great soul music does not require harmonic complexity; instead, it relies on the pocket, the groove, and the human feel of the musicians locking in together.
