# Glenn Zaleski

Glenn Zaleski is a contemporary jazz pianist and educator known for his sophisticated harmonic approach, technical mastery, and dedication to teaching. A faculty member at prestigious jazz institutions, Zaleski has become recognized for his ability to communicate advanced harmonic concepts and his refined approach to piano voicing. His masterclasses and performances demonstrate high-level application of modern jazz harmonic language, serving as inspiration for serious students seeking to deepen their understanding of voicing and harmony. Zaleski represents a lineage of pianists continuing the traditions of harmonic sophistication pioneered by earlier masters.
