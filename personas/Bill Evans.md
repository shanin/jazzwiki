# Bill Evans

Bill Evans was an American pianist and composer known for his harmonic sophistication, lyrical touch, and innovative approach to jazz piano voicing. He revolutionized how jazz pianists voice chords, combining classical music training with modern jazz sensibilities. His influence extends across generations, particularly in the areas of chord voicing, voice leading, and the integration of modal and functional harmony in jazz contexts.
