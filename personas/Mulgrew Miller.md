# Mulgrew Miller

Mulgrew Miller is an accomplished jazz pianist known for his versatility, harmonic sophistication, and ability to accompany a wide range of instrumentalists and singers. Active in jazz since the 1970s, Miller has participated in numerous recordings across multiple styles and has become a respected educator and session musician. His approach to chord voicing and accompaniment represents the continuation and refinement of the language developed by earlier pianists in the post-bop tradition.
