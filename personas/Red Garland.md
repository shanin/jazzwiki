# Red Garland

Red Garland was a pioneering bebop pianist known for his distinctive comping style, rhythmic approach, and innovative harmonic voicing techniques. His playing influenced generations of jazz pianists, and his harmonic and rhythmic innovations became foundational elements of modern jazz piano. His characteristic rhythm patterns, particularly his approach to anticipating chord changes, remain popular reference points for teaching ensemble playing. Garland's voicing approach—particularly his use of spread voicings with consistent perfect fifths from the bass note—exemplifies how strong harmonic foundations can accommodate harmonic tension and serve as pedagogical models.
