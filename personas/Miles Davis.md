# Miles Davis

Miles Davis (1926–1991) was an American jazz trumpeter, bandleader, and composer who is widely recognized as one of the most influential figures in jazz history. His career spanned more than four decades and encompassed nearly every significant development in jazz from bebop through fusion. Davis was known for his innovative approach to rhythm section playing, modal harmony, and electric instrumentation. He led many of the most important ensembles in jazz history and his albums are canonical documents of their respective eras. His leadership style extended to moment-to-moment direction in the studio, as exemplified by his instruction to Red Garland during the 1958 Prestige sessions.
