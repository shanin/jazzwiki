# Bernie Worrell

Bernie Worrell is one of the greatest and most versatile keyboardists of his era, best known for his work with Parliament-Funkadelic. As Parliament's primary keys player, Worrell contributed clavinet, synthesizers, and orchestral arrangements to some of the most innovative funk records ever made. On Mothership Connection, Worrell layers clavinet and synths to create a unified, hypnotic riff that anchors the entire arrangement. His ability to balance technical mastery with absolute pocket discipline made him essential to the Parliament sound and an influence on subsequent generations of keyboardists.
