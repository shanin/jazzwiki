# Winton Marsalis

Winton Marsalis is a renowned jazz trumpet player and educator known for his work in championing and teaching jazz tradition. He is featured prominently in Ken Burns' acclaimed documentary "Jazz," where he explains fundamental concepts of jazz rhythm and feel with exceptional clarity. In particular, Marsalis articulates the importance of the "big four" rhythmic pattern—the moment where jazz rhythm shifted from even march time to syncopated, swinging phrasing. His emphasis on understanding the historical and rhythmic foundations of jazz has made him an important voice in jazz education and advocacy. Marsalis represents the lineage of jazz tradition carried forward into the contemporary era.
