# Tony Williams

Tony Williams (1945–1997) was an American jazz drummer who joined Miles Davis's Second Great Quintet at age 17, transforming what the drum kit could do in a jazz context. His playing was aggressive, polyrhythmic, and interactive in a way that treated the drums as a melodic participant rather than a timekeeper. He later led the fusion group Lifetime.
