# Errol Garner

Errol Garner was a virtuosic jazz pianist and composer celebrated for his distinctive harmonic approach, infectious rhythmic feel, and compositional originality. Known as a self-taught musician with an instinctive approach to harmony, Garner's playing demonstrated that sophisticated voicing concepts—including small six-chord shapes—could be discovered and applied naturally by ear. His influential compositions and recordings exemplify how small chord voicing techniques can produce elegant, singable harmonies. Garner's work serves as proof that mastery of harmonic language does not require traditional theoretical training but rather deep listening and experimentation.
