# Pat Metheny

Pat Metheny (b. 1954) is an American jazz guitarist widely regarded as one of the most versatile and prolific musicians in contemporary jazz. Leader of the Pat Metheny Group, his playing spans lyrical acoustic jazz, ambient soundscapes, and complex rhythmic fusion. He is known for a distinctive warm tone and flowing, long-line melodic improvisation.
