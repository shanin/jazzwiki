# McCoy Tyner

McCoy Tyner is a legendary jazz pianist known for his powerful, percussive touch and innovative approach to voicing and harmony. A key member of John Coltrane's classic quartet, Tyner developed a distinctive piano style that influenced generations of jazz pianists. His approach to rootless voicings, built on fourths and employing quartal harmony, became foundational to modern jazz piano technique and is widely studied and imitated by pianists seeking to develop their harmonic language.
