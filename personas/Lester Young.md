# Lester Young

Lester Young (1909–1959) was a tenor saxophonist whose cool, lyrical approach and innovative phrasing fundamentally changed jazz saxophone in the swing era. Young was famous for his integration of singing and playing, as evidenced by the historical anecdote that he would stop playing mid-solo if he forgot the words he was imagining, illustrating how deeply the vocal melody was intertwined with his musical concept.
