# Lester Young

Lester Young (1909–1959), nicknamed "Pres" (President) by Billie Holiday, was a tenor saxophonist whose cool, understated approach stood in deliberate contrast to the warmer, louder swing style of Coleman Hawkins. His relaxed phrasing, dry tone, and melodic inventiveness were a foundational influence on the cool jazz movement and on virtually every tenor player who followed.
