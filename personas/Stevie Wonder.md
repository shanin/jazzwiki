# Stevie Wonder

Stevie Wonder is a legendary keyboardist, singer, and composer whose mastery of the clavinet and other keyboards defined the sound of soul and funk music from the 1970s onward. Blind from infancy, Wonder developed extraordinary musical intuition and an ability to layer multiple keyboard parts into rich, cohesive arrangements. His work on Superstition is a masterclass in groove playing: the clavinet part contains ghost notes and fills that add human movement and texture while serving the fundamental groove. Wonder's approach to pocket playing—staying in the pocket while adding subtle variations and personality—became a template for keyboardists across genres.
