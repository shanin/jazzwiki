# Peter Martin

Peter Martin is an American jazz pianist and educator, co-founder of Open Studio. A sideman and bandleader with deep roots in the mainstream jazz tradition, he is known for sophisticated harmonic vocabulary and clear, practical pedagogy.

## Appearances

- **[[../videos/-ktm1Q0odH8|Three Jazz Piano Voicing Structures for Lady Bird]]** [▶ 17:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ktm1Q0odH8&t=1023s) — a specific extended voicing (E♭7♯11♭9) is attributed to Martin as an example of the kind of upper-structure sounds the magic voicing system can produce. His broader influence runs through the Open Studio platform that contextualises the lesson.
