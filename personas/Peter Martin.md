# Peter Martin

Peter Martin is a jazz pianist known for his sophisticated voicing approaches and harmonic innovation. His influence extends to other educators and pianists who reference his unconventional voicing choices as models for developing contemporary comping techniques. His approach to upper-structure extensions and altered voicings has become part of the shared vocabulary of modern jazz piano.
