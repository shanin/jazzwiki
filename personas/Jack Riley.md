# Jack Riley

Jack Riley is a music theorist and academic known for his comprehensive analysis of Bill Evans' harmonic approach. His book "The Harmony of Bill Evans" has become a foundational text for jazz pianists seeking to understand how Bill Evans constructed his sophisticated voicings and voice-leading strategies. Riley's clear breakdowns of Evans' harmonic thinking have influenced multiple generations of jazz educators and students.
