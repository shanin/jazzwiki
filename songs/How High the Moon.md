# How High the Moon

A jazz standard composed by Morgan Lewis with lyrics by Nancy Hamilton. How High the Moon is celebrated for its complex harmonic movement and multiple modulations, making it a prime teaching example for recognizing temporary key centers within a single tune. The tune begins in G major but shifts into F major (via G minor to C7 to F major), then to E flat major (via F minor to B flat 7), and back to G major. These modulations are not random; each is a coherent cadence within its new key. Rather than thinking of How High the Moon as dozens of individual chords, musicians who recognize the modulations can simplify the harmonic landscape into three color palettes (G major, F major, E flat major), each offering a single scale to improvise from. This pedagogical approach transforms the tune from technically challenging to conceptually elegant.
