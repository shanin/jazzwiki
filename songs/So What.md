# So What

"So What" is a modal jazz standard from Bill Evans' "Kind of Blue" album, notable for its minimalist approach and modal harmony. The tune's structural simplicity belies its sophisticated voicing possibilities, and its distinctive chord shapes have become templates in jazz piano vocabulary. The tune's influence extends beyond performance to providing voicing models and patterns that jazz pianists reference when constructing new voicing systems.
