# All The Things You Are

A masterpiece of American songwriting composed by Jerome Kern with lyrics by Oscar Hammerstein II, All The Things You Are is renowned for its sophisticated harmonic progressions and modulations. The tune's complex changes and beautiful melody make it a favorite for demonstrating advanced harmonic and voicing concepts. It is essential repertoire for serious jazz musicians.
