# Autumn Leaves

Originally a French song composed by Joseph Kosma with lyrics by Jacques Prévert, Autumn Leaves became a jazz standard with English lyrics by Johnny Mercer. The tune is one of the most frequently played jazz standards, appearing in virtually every jazz musician's repertoire. Its accessible melody and chord changes make it an ideal vehicle for demonstrating various voicing techniques and harmonic concepts.
