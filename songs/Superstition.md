# Superstition

Superstition is a funk-soul classic by Stevie Wonder featuring one of the most iconic clavinet grooves in popular music. The song's main riff, played on clavinet, has a sticky, percussive quality that drives the entire arrangement. What makes the groove deceptively complex is Stevie's use of ghost notes and fills with the left hand to add texture and movement while staying locked in the fundamental rhythm. The groove serves as a foundational study in pocket playing—learning to mimic Stevie's approach requires understanding both the written part and the subtle human variations that make it feel alive.
