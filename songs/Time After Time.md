# Time After Time

A jazz standard used in instructional contexts to demonstrate diatonic seventh chords and secondary dominants. The changes in the opening section of Time After Time are built almost entirely from the key of C major, with chords like A minor seven (vi), D minor seven (ii), G7 (V), B minor seven flat five (vii), and E7 (secondary dominant to vi) forming a coherent harmonic landscape. The tune exemplifies how recognizing diatonic seventh chords and their secondary dominants simplifies improvisation and reveals the underlying structure. Rather than thinking about each chord individually, a musician can identify that the first seven bars are all chords built from C major, making the improvisation a matter of applying the diatonic modes within one key.
