# Days of Wine and Roses

A jazz standard composed by Henry Mancini with lyrics by Johnny Mercer, "Days of Wine and Roses" is one of the most frequently performed jazz ballads. The tune's elegant harmony and melody have made it a staple in jazz repertoire, and its cadences are often adapted by musicians using various harmonic approaches.
