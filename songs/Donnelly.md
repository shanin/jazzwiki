# Donnelly

Donnelly is a bebop composition by Charlie Parker that stands as one of the most instructive pieces in jazz education. Written during the bebop era, the tune integrates advanced harmonic concepts—particularly the use of harmonic minor scales over secondary dominant chords and the voicing structures of broken ninth chords—into a melodic form. Though the solo improvisation is also noteworthy, the composition's real genius lies in the head melody, which serves as a complete harmonic primer for musicians learning to navigate secondary dominants, chromatic alterations, and chord-tone voicings.
