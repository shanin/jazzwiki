Composed by Charlie Parker and recorded in 1947, Billy's Bounce is a classic bebop blues that showcases Parker's ability to navigate harmonic complexity with apparent ease and spontaneity. The head (melody) is relatively straightforward, making it an accessible entry point for transcription study, while the recorded solos exhibit Parker's signature virtuosity and harmonic innovation.
