# On Green Dolphin Street

On Green Dolphin Street is a jazz standard from the film of the same name. The B section of the tune provides particularly rich harmonic content for exploring voicing options and advanced harmony. The progression in E flat major features a mix of major, minor, and dominant chords that work beautifully with sixth diminished scale frameworks. The tune's balance between functional harmony and chromatic color makes it ideal for studying sophisticated voice leading and textural variation.
