# Mothership Connection

Mothership Connection by Parliament is a funky anthem built on a hypnotic riff that loops for several minutes in the live recording. Every instrument in the band—guitar, bass, and keys—plays the same essential riff, creating a unified rhythm section that drives the groove. The original recording features Bernie Worrell on clavinet and synths, layering the keys part with precision. The song exemplifies the Parliament-Funkadelic approach to groove: the riff is the song, and the artistry lies in staying locked in the pocket while the vocals and arrangement shift around it. Extended study of this groove can be transformative for any musician learning to prioritize rhythm section cohesion over individual expression.
