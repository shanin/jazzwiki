A jazz standard composed by Kenny Dorham, Blue Bossa combines bebop sensibility with bossa nova rhythm and Latin influences. The tune's clear harmonic structure and distinctive melody make it a frequent choice for jazz education and jam sessions. Its moderate tempo and accessible chord changes provide excellent opportunities for exploring voicing applications.
