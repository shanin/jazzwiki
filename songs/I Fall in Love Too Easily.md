# I Fall in Love Too Easily

A jazz standard composed by Sammy Fain and Jack Klenner, "I Fall in Love Too Easily" is notable for its melodic beauty and harmonic sophistication. The tune has been arranged and performed by many jazz artists, with Bill Evans' version being particularly influential due to his orchestration of one of its cadences, which demonstrates rich voicing and functional harmony.
