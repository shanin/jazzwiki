# Use Me

Use Me by Bill Withers features one of the most famous clavinet riffs ever recorded, performed by Ray Jackson. The groove is deceptively simple: a two-chord progression (Em7 to A7) played in octaves on clavinet. The simplicity of the written part belies the discipline required to play it authentically—to stay in the pocket without filling space with ghost notes or improvisation, to feel the pocket without embellishing it. The song exemplifies how constraint can deepen musicianship; serving a simple groove with complete commitment and feel is more valuable than adding complexity.
