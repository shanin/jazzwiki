# You're My Everything

You're My Everything is a jazz standard recorded famously by the Miles Davis Quintet during the 1958 Prestige Sessions. The tune served as the vehicle for Red Garland's iconic piano intro, which demonstrates the pianist's distinctive approach to spread voicings and block chord comping. The ballad is notable for its intimate studio context, particularly the preserved false start that captures the band's natural interplay and Miles Davis's direct arrangement instructions.
