# My Foolish Heart

A jazz standard composed by Victor Young with lyrics by Jack Klenner, "My Foolish Heart" is a classic ballad known for its beautiful melody and harmonic structure. The tune is frequently used in jazz contexts as a vehicle for exploring harmonic sophistication and is particularly suitable for demonstrating rich voicing approaches.
