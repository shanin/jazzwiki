# What Are Sugar Chords?

**Presenter:** Adam Maness | **Channel:** Open Studio
**Duration:** 17:32 | **YouTube:** [▶ Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DoOTYEpdYJM)

## Overview

Sugar chords are a systematic approach to creating rich, smooth harmonic textures by combining three distinct voicing structures: closed voicings, drop two, and drop three. This lesson teaches how to mix and match these structures across jazz standards to achieve beautiful voice leading and contrary motion while maintaining flexibility across different registers and contexts.

## Practice Notes

The lesson uses the B section of On Green Dolphin Street in E flat major. All voicing examples are built from sixth diminished scale fragments. When practicing, focus on the structural differences between the three voicing types: closed voicings keep all chord tones tightly packed; drop two creates a wide spread with the second-highest note moved down an octave; drop three uses an even wider spread with the third-highest note lowered. Start with each structure individually, then experiment with mixing them to create counter-melodies and contrary motion in the inner voices. The goal is not rigid adherence to a formula, but building intuition for voice leading and textural variety.
