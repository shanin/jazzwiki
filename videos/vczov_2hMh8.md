# 5 Easy Jazz Piano Chords That Sound Great

**Presenter:** Peter Martin | **Channel:** Open Studio
**Duration:** 9:54 | **YouTube:** [▶ Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vczov_2hMh8)

## Overview

Peter Martin introduces five essential jazz piano chord voicings that avoid the hollow sound of root position chords. The lesson focuses on the Root Shell Pretty framework—a three-layer approach (root, shell, and pretty notes) that professional jazz musicians use to create warm, sophisticated voicings across major 7, minor 7, dominant 7, and altered dominant chords.

## Practice Notes

All five chords follow the same three-layer construction from bottom to top: root in the left hand, shell (third and seventh) in the left hand, and two pretty notes in the right hand. Learn each voicing thoroughly in C major, then practice moving them chromatically (half step by half step) to develop facility in all keys. Alternatively, practice through the circle of 5ths or circle of 4ths for variety. The final exercise combines chord 4 (C7 sharp 11) and chord 5 (C7 sharp 9) with voice leading in the right hand to introduce basic progression technique.
